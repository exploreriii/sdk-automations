# 8.2 First effects

> **Draft, written 2026-09-02 ahead of the stage E build landing.** Review the
> honest-scope notes in Setup before running; the build SHA is recorded at
> step 1.

**Question this can falsify:** the build plan's FX gate — that one managed
comment and one mapped label land on the sandbox for real, verifiably,
idempotently, and reversibly; that the kill switch fences an armed writer;
and that a maintainer's manual reversal is never fought. D125's marker,
D12's identity rules, D46's read-backs, and the stage C journal loop are all
on trial with live GitHub as the counterparty.

## Setup

- The 8.1 sandbox and store, continued — the same shell lineage, restarted
  with `APP_SLUG` so writes are armed. The restart is performed with SIGTERM
  and doubles as 8.1 step 7's drill (exit 0, one shutdown line, no stranded
  claim): two protocols, one boundary, both recorded.
- The sandbox config flips to `mode: active` for the session (intake
  enabled, `announce: true`, `awaitingTriage` mapped) and back to the 8.1
  config at close. Binding is next-delivery (8.1 step 2's evidence).
- **Honest scope.** Three of the gate's behaviors are proven by the injected
  suite rather than live, because live timing cannot reliably hit them: the
  three crash windows, the D12 changed-body update, and the sentUnknown
  resolution. The live steps below cover what only live GitHub can prove —
  real writes, real authorship, real idempotence against the ledger, real
  human reversal — and step 7 attempts one live approximation of a crash
  mid-apply, recorded whichever way the timing lands.

## Steps

1. **Arm.** SIGTERM the 8.1 shell (record 8.1 step 7's evidence), restart
   with `APP_SLUG`. Startup line must say writes are armed. Record the SHA.
2. **Activate.** Commit the active config; verify next-delivery binding.
3. **The two effects.** Open a fresh issue. Expect, in order, in the log:
   the decision with two approved effects, journal intent → done per call,
   and on GitHub: the `awaitingTriage` label applied (two-call plan if a
   position was displaced) and an App-authored comment whose marker parses
   as v1 with the right capability, kind, and effect digest. Record the
   comment's author login (`<slug>[bot]`) and both journal rows.
4. **Idempotence against the ledger.** Redeliver the `issues.opened` from
   the App's Recent Deliveries. Expect `deliveryDuplicate`, zero new writes
   (comment count and label set unchanged), journal untouched.
5. **Already-path.** Open a second issue; after its effects land, redeliver
   ITS opened event too. Same expectation — the duplicate guard, not the
   applier, is what answers; the journal shows one done set per effect.
6. **Kill switch on an armed writer.** Restart with `KILL_SWITCH=1` (second
   SIGTERM boundary). Open a third issue: the decision must refuse
   uniformly with `killSwitch`, and GitHub must show ZERO writes for it.
   Restore.
7. **Live crash approximation.** With active restored, open two issues in
   quick succession and `kill -9` the shell the moment the first
   `deliveryClaimed` appears. On restart: any journal row left open must
   resolve through the sweep's read-back path (present → done, absent →
   one resend), and the delivery lane recovers as in 8.1. Record what the
   timing actually produced — an open row resolved is the prize; a clean
   miss is still evidence the windows are narrow.
8. **Reversal, unfought.** As the maintainer: delete the App's comment on
   issue one and remove its label. Expect NOTHING: no re-add without a new
   triggering event, for at least two sweep intervals — the no-background-
   repair promise observed. The journal's done rows still hold the receipt.
9. **Close.** GitHub-side audit: App-authored actions over the window equal
   the journal's done rows exactly, in count and target. Config restored;
   the session's writes enumerated in the observations; store totals clean.

The FX gate, mapped: step 3 lands both effect types with verified identity;
steps 4–5 prove idempotence against GitHub's own ledger; step 6 demonstrates
the kill switch on an armed writer; the injected suite plus step 7 stand in

step 8 is the tested reversal; step 9 closes with zero unexplained writes.
Pass once, on a reviewed build, with every observation cited.
